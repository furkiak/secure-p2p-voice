Before you share a code

Know the boundaries.

  • No app signaling serverYou carry offer and answer codes yourself. The service used to send them may keep logs.
  • Network assistanceThis preview uses Google's public STUN service. Peers and STUN can observe IP-related metadata; no TURN fallback is configured.
  • Two encryption layersA password-derived AES-GCM key protects shared codes. WebRTC uses DTLS-SRTP for media. A weak or leaked password still matters.
  • Connection limitsSome networks need TURN and will fail here. End call stops local tracks; a recipient can still retain audio already received.
